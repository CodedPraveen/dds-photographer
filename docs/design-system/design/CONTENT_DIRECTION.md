# Purpose

This document defines how the website communicates with visitors.

While the design creates the visual experience, content builds trust, tells stories, and encourages visitors to take action.

Every word on the website should feel intentional.

---

# Vision

The website should communicate confidence through simplicity.

We don't sell photography with long paragraphs.

We allow photographs to tell most of the story, while written content provides clarity, trust, and direction.

The content should feel:

- Professional
- Honest
- Calm
- Elegant
- Human
- Memorable

---

# Content Philosophy

**Less words. More meaning.**

Every sentence should provide value.

If removing a sentence does not reduce understanding, it probably should not exist.

---

# Writing Principles

Every piece of content should follow these principles.

## 1. Clear

Visitors should immediately understand the message.

Avoid confusing language.

---

## 2. Simple

Write naturally.

Avoid complicated words when simple ones communicate the same meaning.

---

## 3. Human

Write like a real person.

Avoid sounding robotic or overly corporate.

---

## 4. Authentic

Never exaggerate.

Never overpromise.

Trust is more valuable than marketing language.

---

## 5. Emotional

Photography captures emotions.

The writing should support those emotions rather than replace them.

---

## 6. Timeless

Avoid trendy internet phrases that quickly become outdated.

Write content that remains relevant for years.

---

# Brand Voice

The website speaks with confidence but never arrogance.

Characteristics:

- Calm
- Professional
- Friendly
- Honest
- Respectful
- Creative
- Experienced

Avoid sounding:

- Salesy
- Aggressive
- Overly casual
- Overly technical

---

# Tone of Voice

The tone should adapt depending on the section.

| Section | Tone |
|----------|------|
| Hero | Inspiring |
| About | Personal |
| Services | Informative |
| Portfolio | Minimal |
| Testimonials | Authentic |
| Contact | Friendly |

---

# Headlines

Headlines should be:

- Short
- Strong
- Easy to remember

Prefer:

```
Every Story Matters.

Moments Worth Remembering.

Captured With Purpose.

Real Emotions.

Your Story.
```

Avoid:

```
Welcome To My Amazing Photography Website

We Offer The Best Photography Services For Everyone

Professional Photography Services Since 2015
```

---

# Paragraphs

Keep paragraphs short.

Recommended:

- 2–4 lines
- One idea per paragraph
- Easy to scan

Avoid long blocks of text.

---

# Calls To Action (CTA)

CTAs should be clear and direct.

Examples:

- View Portfolio
- Explore Stories
- Contact Now
- Let's Talk
- Get in Touch

Avoid:

- Click Here
- Learn More
- Submit
- Continue

---

# About Section Content

The goal is to build trust.

Focus on:

- Passion
- Experience
- Philosophy
- Style
- Approach

Avoid writing a complete life story.

---

# Portfolio Content

Photography should communicate first.

Content should remain minimal.

Include only useful information:

- Project Name
- Category
- Location
- Year
- Short Description (optional)

Avoid long project articles unless necessary.

---

# Services Content

Describe:

- What is offered
- Who it is for
- What clients receive

Keep descriptions concise.

Visitors should understand the service within a few seconds.

---

# Testimonials

Testimonials should feel authentic.

Display:

- Client Name
- Event or Project
- Short Review

Avoid editing reviews into overly polished marketing copy.

---

# Contact Content

Make contacting the photographer feel easy.

Use encouraging language.

Examples:

- Let's create something memorable.
- I'd love to hear your story.
- Ready to plan your next shoot?

Avoid pressure-based wording.

---

# Microcopy

Small pieces of text matter.

Examples include:

- Button labels
- Form placeholders
- Empty states
- Loading messages
- Error messages

They should be:

- Friendly
- Clear
- Helpful

Example:

Instead of:

```
Error
```

Use:

```
Something went wrong.
Please try again.
```

---

# SEO Content

Write for humans first.

Search engines second.

Never stuff keywords into paragraphs.

Good content naturally improves SEO.

---

# Grammar

Always use:

- Proper spelling
- Proper punctuation
- Consistent capitalization

Avoid inconsistent writing styles across pages.

---

# Numbers

Prefer readable formats.

Examples:

```
10+

5 Years

100+

24/7
```

Be consistent throughout the website.

---

# Content Hierarchy

Every page should follow the same reading flow.

```
Headline

↓

Supporting Text

↓

Primary Action

↓

Additional Information
```

Visitors should never feel overwhelmed.

---

# Accessibility

Content should be easy to understand.

Guidelines:

- Short sentences
- Clear language
- Descriptive links
- Meaningful button text
- Avoid jargon

---

# Content Review Checklist

Before publishing content, verify:

- Is it clear?
- Is it concise?
- Is it useful?
- Does it sound human?
- Does it match the brand voice?
- Does it support the photography?
- Is it grammatically correct?
- Is it easy to scan?

If any answer is **No**, revise the content.

---

# Things We Avoid

The project intentionally avoids:

- Buzzwords
- Marketing clichés
- Long introductions
- Keyword stuffing
- Generic stock phrases
- Excessive punctuation
- ALL CAPS WRITING
- Clickbait language
- Emoji-heavy content
- AI-sounding copy

Examples to avoid:

- We are the best.
- World-class photography.
- Unmatched quality.
- Premium solutions.
- Innovative excellence.

These statements are generic and do not build trust.

---

# Success Criteria

The content is successful when visitors:

- Understand the photographer quickly.
- Feel connected to the work.
- Easily navigate the website.
- Trust the brand.
- Know what to do next.
- Contact the photographer with confidence.

---

# Guiding Question

Before adding any text, ask:

> Does this sentence help the visitor understand, trust, or take action?

If not, remove it.
