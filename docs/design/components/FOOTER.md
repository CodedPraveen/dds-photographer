# Footer Section

The footer serves as the final touchpoint of the website, providing visitors with a clean conclusion to their browsing experience while encouraging them to stay connected.

The design should remain minimal, premium, and consistent with the overall visual language of the website.

---

## Newsletter Section

Positioned at the top of the footer is a simple newsletter subscription area.

Rather than using aggressive marketing language, the copy should feel personal and aligned with the photographer's brand.

```text
────────────────────────────────────────

        Stay Connected

Receive updates about recent projects,
photography stories, and future sessions.

[ Your Email Address                 ]

          Subscribe

────────────────────────────────────────
```

The newsletter should only request an email address to keep the interaction simple and frictionless.

## Newsletter Interaction

The subscription form should feel smooth and responsive.

### Input

- Soft rounded corners
- Premium border
- Clear placeholder text
- Subtle focus animation

### Subscribe Button

On hover:

- Slight elevation
- Smooth color transition
- Gentle scale effect

After successful subscription:

```text
✓ Thank you for subscribing!

You'll receive updates about future projects.
```

The success message replaces the input momentarily before returning to its default state.

---

## Background Style

The footer should visually separate itself from the rest of the website without feeling disconnected.

Suggested styling:

- Slightly darker background than the main site
- Soft gradient or subtle texture
- Generous vertical spacing
- Thin divider line separating the portfolio section and footer

---

## Design Principles

- Clean and uncluttered layout
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Plenty of whitespace
- Minimal text
- Elegant typography
- Smooth hover animations
- Mobile-friendly responsive layout

The footer should feel like the natural conclusion of the photographer's story, leaving visitors with a professional impression while providing an easy way to stay connected through the newsletter and social platforms.
