## Design Direction

Since the website uses a **3D camera as the primary storytelling element**, the portfolio should avoid a perfectly symmetrical layout.

Instead, adopt a **slightly asymmetrical editorial composition**, inspired by premium magazines and luxury photography books.

This approach creates a curated and artistic presentation rather than resembling a conventional image gallery or e-commerce product grid.

The layout should feel intentional, spacious, and visually balanced while allowing each photograph to stand out.

The overall goal is to create a premium exhibition-like experience where visitors naturally explore the photographer's work instead of simply browsing a collection of images.
